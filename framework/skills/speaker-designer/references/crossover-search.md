# Search and diagnose a realized crossover

Read for topology changes, component reduction, stock fitting or a close ranking. The built-in `crossover_fit` provides bounded least squares, on-axis ablation and joint stock trials. The wider checks below are agent/project work, not features to assume the app implements.

## Search the user's problem

Record the user's priorities and component budget separately from diagnostic thresholds. Cover the full band the brief describes: peaks beside dips, top-octave contour above narrow-band metric limits, bass balance, sustained extension, sensitivity, output and relevant listening-window/in-room behavior. Explicit protective conditions cannot be bought back by a better weighted score. A breakup-rejection requirement needs its own branch leakage check; a smooth summed response can conceal leakage.

Before a batch, score several contrasting networks, not only the reference against itself. Check the winner's tradeoffs and verify any project-library calculation against a standalone app run. Freeze the score and stock-realization method; if either changes, rescore/refit all affected rows, including former losers. Construct trade tables from the full feasible population. Preference predictions and designer tolerances support interpretation of the brief; they do not replace it.

## Explain the incumbent before replacing it

For fixed drivers, first remove each incumbent element with other values fixed. Read branch leakage, crossing, relative phase, full-band sum and angular behavior. Then refit promising omissions and materialize them on real stock. Fixed-value removal tests function in this circuit; refitting tests whether the other elements can recover it. Neither proves a universal minimum part count. Estimate grid combinations and screen topology families coarsely before expensive runs.

If the user requests a build-up from zero, simulate the zero-part state. Add one part for a named residual and compare against omitting it while holding previous values, sources, position and polarity fixed. Record subsequent refits or geometry changes as separate operations. Preserve an unsuccessful path as evidence about that path, not every possible network.

## Stock and optimization

Derive stock ranges from this driver's impedance, desired shelf, notch frequencies and level mismatch. Check catalogue coverage and internal gaps as well as endpoints; an inherited list's endpoint is not a manufacturer's limit. Investigate meaningful boundary optima before claiming infeasibility. Keep electrical losses, price and package dimensions attached to one exact SKU; do not combine attributes from different coil shapes or wire gauges.

Compare continuous and realized values. For sparse series, fit with the critical part held at actual stock, or evaluate joint combinations bracketing the fitted values and refine several promising combinations. Independent nearest snapping can miss interactions. Read the evaluated versus total combination counts; bounded stock enumeration can leave unexplored combinations.

Test a fitter on the known incumbent and a perturbed start, retaining the best observed feasible point, including the initial point. Termination on step tolerance is not evidence of a good fit. Crossing detectors and hinge terms can make an objective nonsmooth; consider smooth surrogates or an incumbent-seeded population method and repeated seeds for close leaders. A global optimizer is not proof of a global optimum, and local fitting is distinct from a population search. `network_fit` adds general-network targets and explicit numeric constraints; its trial table supports the agent's interpretation.

## Realized protection

First distinguish a manufacturer's minimum protective filter, stated operating band and power-test condition. Derive the applicable check from that evidence and the brief, not another speaker's crossover. Test actual loaded driver-terminal transfer on the scoring grid, including pad and impedance, rather than LC products, element bounds or acoustic crossing alone. Keep absolute terminal voltage at equal amplifier input separate from attenuation normalized to the driver's passband; state any compensation to equal listening level.

Apply genuine required protection as a feasibility screen in search and final selection. Give coarse screens margin and rerun the shortlist on the final app/grid. If a free fit defeats the protective high pass, consider fixing a damped protective section and fitting the remaining shaping, then test whether the acoustic target is still reachable. Do not transfer a published endurance rating to an unlike network without evidence. This check predicts linear drive, not endurance or distortion.

## Diagnose before attributing

At the frequency and observation angle causing a feature, inspect complex section contributions and reconstruct the sum. Compare: ideal acoustic targets at actual positions (geometry), real section magnitudes aligned in phase (available magnitude), and the real network (phase and leakage). These are diagnostic counterfactuals, not automatically realizable designs. A dominant driver's trough cannot be filled by adjusting the phase of a negligible second branch. Test equalization with the rest fixed and inspect both shoulders and the whole relevant band. Read valley depth beside window width and flag piston-based breakup predictions as uncertain.

Before selection, compare close alternatives under the same stock, protection, tolerance, source and angular assumptions. Carry the evidence and the user's priority into the ranking, rather than choosing by the number of designer-defined threshold misses.
