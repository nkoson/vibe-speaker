## Observation
The final ranking preferred the candidate with the best sensitivity, midband smoothness and vertical behaviour, while its system f3 was about 112 Hz against a 38 Hz target. Another candidate reached about 56 Hz at the cost of 6 dB of sensitivity and a poor vertical window. The brief named bass extension as central. The selected design was called the best candidate and received the full documentation package.

## Why it mattered
The user received an elaborate package for a speaker that does not do what was asked. The evaluation table showed the miss clearly; the ranking narrative weighed it like any other row.

## Advice
Before ranking, consider asking which objectives the brief treats as the point of the speaker and whether any candidate misses one of them by a margin that no later refinement can close. If so, say plainly that no candidate meets the brief and rank on how close each comes to the central objectives, not on the sum of secondary merits. It can be the right call to recommend one more iteration on a different concept, or to return to the user with the tradeoff, instead of selecting. A smooth midband is not a reason to document a speaker without bass.
